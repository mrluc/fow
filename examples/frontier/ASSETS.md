# Artwork and terrain

All current visuals were made for this example on 2026-11-02. There are no remaining Kenney sprites in the app. The original source PNGs are kept at full generated resolution, with genuine alpha transparency; CSS scales them to their display sizes. Both character frames have matching 1199×1312 canvases.

## Procedural terrain

`priv/static/images/terrain.png` and `priv/static/css/terrain.css` are generated by `scripts/generate-terrain.cjs`. The source is deterministic multi-octave value noise, seeded at 0xF09, with irregular dirt paths, fine grain and sparse grass tips. One continuous field is sampled across the whole world, avoiding seams. It is displayed as 144 subtiles, nine per location. No image generator, external texture pack or third-party runtime dependency is involved.

## Generated cutouts

Built-in ChatGPT image generation was used, with `transparent_background: true`. The standing scout was the reference/edit target for its walking frame. PNGs were copied unchanged into `priv/static/images/`. No API key or fallback CLI was used. The following prompts are the production briefs used for the outputs; the walking edit additionally supplied the standing image as its reference.

### fog-of-war-logo.png

Use case: logo-brand. Create an original fantasy strategy game emblem, transparent background. Large exact lettering 'FoW' in sculpted, bevelled antique gold with dark blue stone inset and restrained ornamental metal frame. Underneath, a small curved parchment/metal banner with exact text 'FOG OF WAR'. Classical three-dimensional painted fantasy MMO title-screen feel inspired by World of Warcraft's genre, but an original silhouette and ornamentation, no copied Warcraft insignias. Front-facing, compact horizontal emblem, no scene behind it. Beautiful readable lettering at small header size. Genuine transparent cutout, no background rectangle or fake checkerboard.

### pine.png

Use case: stylized-concept. A single evergreen pine tree game sprite, isolated on genuine transparent background. Isometric 2:1 RTS camera, looking down about 30 degrees. Late-1990s hand-painted strategy art, richly shaded clustered deep green needles, warm brown trunk, Warcraft II / Age of Empires II level of readable retro detail. Full tree centered, generous transparent margin, no ground tile, no scenery, no text, no checkerboard, no huge cast shadow. Light from upper left. Attractive naturally irregular silhouette. This must function as a small 80px tall cutout sprite on procedural grass.

### scout-idle.png

Use case: stylized-concept. One full-body human scout game sprite on genuine transparent background. Original late-1990s painted isometric RTS style similar in level of detail to Age of Empires II. Young adventurer in muted ochre tunic, moss green short cloak, brown boots, small leather satchel, no weapon. Isometric camera looking down 30 degrees, facing southeast (toward viewer's lower right). STANDING idle pose, feet on the same ground plane, arms relaxed. Entire character centered, enough transparent space all around for a second walking pose later. Crisp coherent silhouette readable at 48px tall, upper-left lighting. No ground, no scenery, no text, no panels, no checkerboard. One character only.

### scout-walk.png

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit this transparent scout game sprite into ONE second animation frame. Preserve the same character identity, face, clothing, colors, upper-left lighting, painted retro RTS style, camera view, exact canvas dimensions, head position and overall scale. Change only pose: a modest walking step toward lower right, front leg a little forward, opposite arm subtly forward, cloak slightly shifted. Keep feet grounded on the same bottom baseline and body centered exactly like the original, so CSS can alternate these two PNGs without jumping. Not an action leap, not a new design. Genuine transparent background, no scene, no text, no extra panels. Entire character within original framing.

The scout idle and walking PNGs were subsequently mirrored horizontally, including the top-level copies, originals, and 512px/256px variants. This changes their facing direction without regenerating the artwork.
